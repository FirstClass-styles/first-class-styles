Helpful Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about appointments, deposits, services, and visiting the salon.

What days is the salon open?

First Class Styles is open Saturday through Tuesday, 9 AM–4 PM. The salon is closed Wednesday through Friday.

How far ahead should I book?

Please submit your appointment request at least 24 hours before your preferred start time. Available dates and times are shown in the booking calendar.

How long is an appointment?

Each appointment is scheduled for four hours. Available start times are shown in the booking planner.

Is a deposit required?

Yes. A $25 non-refundable deposit is required. Your request remains pending until the salon verifies the deposit and confirms your appointment.

How do I pay the deposit?

Please contact the salon through WhatsApp for the current deposit instructions before sending payment. The remaining balance is paid in cash at the salon.

What services are available?

Closure Sew-In, Quick Weave, Signature Sew-In with Leave Out, Signature Shampoo & Blow Dry, and Signature Silk Press. Contact the salon for service pricing.

Where is First Class Styles?

3813 Old Silver Hill Rd, Suitland, MD 20746.

How do I contact the salon?

Call or WhatsApp +1 (240) 370-1128, or email firstclass.styles1@gmail.com.
